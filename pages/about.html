About

Soheil Zarrinpour is an Iranian-American composer working across electronic, electroacoustic, and acoustic media. His music draws on Persian musical traditions, algorithmic processes, and immersive spatialization. He is currently based in Tuscaloosa, Alabama.

Selected Achievements

Soheil's electroacoustic works have been presented at major international festivals. His piece has been accepted for performance at the New York City Electroacoustic Music Festival (NYCEMF) 2026, one of the largest showcases of electroacoustic music in the world. His work Trapped Wind was selected for the Gilgamesh International Music Festival 2025.

He is a two-time recipient of the Reza Korourian Awards for Electroacoustic Music, earning 3rd Place in 2021 and being named a Selected Composer in 2022. He has participated in composition workshops at the Tehran International Electronic Music Festival (TIEMF) with composers including Dimitri Papageorgiou and Joachim Heintz.

Publications

His piece The Order of Percussion was published on the CD Electrominimal by Contemporary Music Records in 2024. Resurrection was released on the CD Phantasma by Yarava Music Group in 2021.

Collaborations & Affiliations

As an arranger and producer, he has collaborated with renowned Iranian traditional singer Salar Aghili. He has studied composition in masterclass settings with Ken Ueno, Nina C. Young, Chaya Czernowin, Don Freund, Arshia Samsaminia, Reza Vali, and Ahmad Pejman.

Soheil is a member of the Birmingham Art Music Alliance.
