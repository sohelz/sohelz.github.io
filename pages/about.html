About

Soheil Zarrinpour is a Persian composer working across electronic, electroacoustic, and acoustic media. His music draws on Persian musical traditions, algorithmic processes, and immersive spatialization.

He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Piano Performance from Tehran University of Art, where he studied composition with Arman Norouzi and Dr. Madjid Tahriri. He is currently pursuing a Master of Music in Composition at The University of Alabama on a full tuition scholarship (2024–2026), studying with Dr. Tyler Bradley Walker and Dr. Amir Zaheri. He has received a full Graduate Teaching Assistantship from the University of Iowa, where he begins a PhD in Composition in 2026, studying with Dr. Jean-François Charles.

Selected Achievements

His electroacoustic works have been presented at major international festivals. His piece has been accepted for performance at the New York City Electroacoustic Music Festival (NYCEMF) 2026, one of the largest showcases of electroacoustic music in the world. His work Trapped Wind was selected for the Gilgamesh International Music Festival 2025.

He is a two-time recipient of the Reza Korourian Awards for Electroacoustic Music, earning 3rd Place in 2021 and being named a Selected Composer in 2022. He has participated in composition workshops at the Tehran International Electronic Music Festival (TIEMF) with Joachim Heintz and Dimitri Papageorgiou.

Publications

His piece The Order of Percussion was published on the CD Electrominimal by Contemporary Music Records in 2024. Resurrection was released on the CD Phantasma by Yarava Music Group in 2021.

Affiliations

Soheil is a member of the Birmingham Art Music Alliance.
