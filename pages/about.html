About

Soheil Zarrinpour is a composer, researcher, and developer specializing in electroacoustic music, accessibility in music technology, and Persian musical traditions.

He is currently completing a Master of Music in Composition at the University of Alabama, where he works as a Graduate Teaching Assistant.

Research Interests

His research spans ear training for electronic music composers, accessibility in music technology, and the integration of Persian musical traditions into electroacoustic composition.

Awards

Reza Korourian Awards for Electroacoustic Music. First place in a National Music Composition Competition.
